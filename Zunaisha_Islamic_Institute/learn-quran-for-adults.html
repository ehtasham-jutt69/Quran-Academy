Reconnecting with the Quran in Adulthood

Many adult Muslims carry a quiet sense of regret that they did not learn to read the Holy Quran proficiently in their youth, or that years away from regular practice have eroded their fluency. Attending a local class full of young children can feel uncomfortable or embarrassing.

At Zunaisha Islamic Institute, we believe every adult deserves an encouraging, mature, and patient learning environment. Our teachers treat adult students with utmost respect as pair learners. There are no exams or peer pressure—only methodical, private 1-on-1 instruction focused on your personal spiritual goals.

Learning Tracks Tailored for Adults

Beginner: Arabic Reading from Scratch

Accelerated Noorani Qaida designed for mature minds. Adults understand phonetic rules quickly and typically finish in 6 to 8 weeks.

Intermediate: Pronunciation Correction

Unlearning habitual errors, perfecting tough Arabic throat letters (Ayn, Haa, Qaf), and mastering smooth breath control.

Advanced: Tajweed with Meaning (Tafseer)

Reciting with professional Tajweed while learning word-by-word vocabulary and the historical context of the verses you read.

Daily Salah & Essential Duas

Ensuring your five daily prayers (Salah), Tashahhud, and Surah Al-Fatihah are recited with 100% precision.
