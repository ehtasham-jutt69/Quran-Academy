Why Many Sisters and Parents Prefer Female Quran Tutors

For many Muslim sisters, finding a qualified female Quran teacher locally in non-Muslim countries can be remarkably challenging. Most local mosques only have male Imams available, leaving adult women and teenage girls without accessible mentorship. Furthermore, many sisters feel natural hesitation reciting out loud in front of a male instructor, especially when correcting delicate vocal sounds (such as Ghunnah, Madd, and Makharij).

Similarly, parents of young children—especially toddlers and sensitive beginners—find that female teachers often provide a maternal, patient, and soothing presence that reduces class anxiety. At Zunaisha Islamic Institute, we have cultivated a team of accredited female Islamic educators (Ustadhas) who are graduates of Al-Azhar and recognized Islamic universities, holding Ijazahs in Hafs 'an 'Asim.
