Who We Are

Bridging Heritage and the Modern Classroom

Zunaisha Islamic Institute Ltd is an educational institute incorporated in England and Wales (Company Registration No. 16378523). Our registered office is located at 124 City Road, London EC1V 2NX, United Kingdom.

Unlike unregistered freelance websites, we operate under strict UK corporate transparency and GDPR data protection laws. Every curriculum, tutor vetting procedure, and billing mechanism is governed by formal quality assurance standards to ensure parents and adult students receive world-class education with absolute peace of mind.

Our Core Pillars

Scholarly Authenticity (Sanad)

Our instructors are graduates of prestigious Islamic universities (such as Al-Azhar) holding continuous recitation chains (Ijazah) connecting back to the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ).

Child Safeguarding & Vetting

All teachers undergo identity checks, background screening, and child safety training before being assigned young students.

Accessibility Worldwide

Round-the-clock lesson availability accommodating families across the UK, USA, Canada, Australia, and Europe in their local time zones and preferred currencies.

Our Child Safeguarding & Protection Policy

We take child protection with utmost seriousness. In full alignment with UK statutory guidelines, we enforce a strict Code of Conduct: classes are recorded for academic quality audits, parents are always welcome in sessions, and our faculty adheres to positive encouragement techniques. Shouting, harsh reprimands, or inappropriate conduct results in immediate termination and reporting.
