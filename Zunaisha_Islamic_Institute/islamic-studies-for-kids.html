Nurturing Muslim Children in the Modern Diaspora

Raising confident practicing Muslim children in the UK, USA, Canada, and Australia requires more than just teaching them to read the Arabic script phonetically. Children need to understand why we pray, who Allah is, why Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ) is our ultimate role model, and how to carry themselves with dignity, kindness, and moral excellence at school and in their neighborhoods.

At Zunaisha Islamic Institute, our Islamic Studies for Kids program presents Islamic teachings in a warm, relatable, and joyful manner. We replace dry lecturing with interactive digital storytelling, visual quizzes, and open discussion where children feel safe asking questions about their faith.

5 Core Pillars of Our Kids Islamic Syllabus

1. Practical Salah (Prayer) & Wudu

Step-by-step physical postures (Qiyam, Ruku, Sujood), correct recitation of Tashahhud, Durood Ibrahim, and Dua Qunoot with meanings.

2. Daily Sunnah Duas & 6 Kalimas

Memorizing everyday supplications: before eating, sleeping, entering the bathroom, leaving home, and boarding a vehicle.

3. Stories of the Prophets (Qisas Al-Anbiya)

Engaging stories from Adam, Nuh, Ibrahim, Musa, and Isa (AS) through the noble life (Seerah) of Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ).

4. Islamic Ethics & Manners (Akhlaaq)

Developing honesty, truthfulness, respecting parents and teachers, controlling anger, and being a kind, compassionate friend.
